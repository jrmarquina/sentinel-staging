Hurricane
Preparedness
Head Start Centers in Puerto Rico

Use this checklist to verify the readiness of your facilities, staff, equipment, and documentation before a storm or hurricane makes landfall.

Emergency plan updated and distributed to staff.
Critical documents backed up and secured (physical and digital).
Employee, family, and supplier contact information verified.
IT and electronic equipment secured.
Emergency communication procedures tested.
Outdoor play areas inspected and secured.
Roof, doors, windows, and drainage inspection completed.
Loose objects removed or secured.
Generators inspected and fuel available.
Vehicles and transportation prepared for emergencies.
Potable water and emergency supplies inventory updated.
Alarm and security systems verified.
Food protected and properly stored.
Staff training completed.
Reopening procedures defined.
Post-event inspection planned.
